# Rateqo — Brand
- **Name:** Rateqo (one word, capital R, lowercase qo)
- **Tagline:** "Rateqo. The firm quote, minus the desk."
- **Why:** **Rate + qo** — *qo* from **quote**. The product's whole job is the quote: it gets a firm, executable FX quote from Textile's RFQ market instead of a negotiated OTC-desk price, proves the edge in its decision log, and settles it. Six letters, no dictionary word, no existing product.
- **Name check (2026-10-22):** verified unused — exact web search for "Rateqo" returned zero hits, GitHub repo search for `rateqo` was empty (`ahmardchain/rateqo` free), and npm returned 404 for the name. Not a trademark clearance — re-check before any public launch beyond the hackathon.
- **History:** briefly named RateSabi at first scaffold (2026-10-22); renamed to Rateqo the same day for uniqueness (RateSabi collided with SabioTrade / SAB-token noise in search).
- **Palette:** Ink `#070b12` (background), Panel `#0c1320`, Edge `#1c2a44`, Mint `#35e0b0` (primary / go), Gold `#f5c04e` (accents / prizes / caution), Muted `#8fa3bf`.
- **Voice:** Short, plain, receipts-first. Numbers before adjectives. Never claim a quote, user, or payment that didn't happen — say "indicative", "dry-run", or "wallet-gated" when that's the truth.
