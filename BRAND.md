# Zelvo — Brand
- **Name:** Zelvo (one word, capital Z).
- **Tagline:** "The firm quote, minus the desk."
- **The product in one line:** a treasury agent for Nigerian earners in USD₮/USDC spending cNGN. It gets a firm, executable FX quote from Textile's RFQ market instead of a negotiated OTC-desk price, proves the edge in its decision log, and settles on Celo.
- **Palette:** Ink `#070b12` (background), Panel `#0c1320`, Edge `#1c2a44`, Mint `#35e0b0` (primary / go), Gold `#f5c04e` (accents / prizes / caution), Muted `#8fa3bf`.
- **Voice:** Short, plain, receipts-first. Numbers before adjectives. Never claim a quote, user, or payment that didn't happen — say "indicative", "dry-run", or "wallet-gated" when that's the truth.

## History & name-collision note (honest record, 2026-10-22)
- This project was renamed twice on 2026-10-22: **RateSabi → Rateqo → Zelvo**.
  - *RateSabi* (first scaffold) was dropped because it collided with SabioTrade / SAB-token noise in search.
  - *Rateqo* (Rate + qo/quote, 6 letters) was verified fully unused on 2026-10-22 — exact web search returned zero hits, GitHub repo search was empty, npm returned 404. It was replaced anyway.
  - *Zelvo* was chosen by Ahmard, the project owner, as the final name.
- **Known collision, disclosed:** the name Zelvo is NOT unique. A live personal-finance app already uses the name Zelvo (multi-currency accounts, budgets, a "Zelvo Money Buddy"), and there is also a Zelvo herbal brand and a Zelvo energy company. This collision was found during the 2026-10-22 name check and shown to the owner, who chose Zelvo anyway. Recorded here so the choice is on the record, not an oversight.
- **Implication:** this is not a trademark clearance. Anyone searching "Zelvo" may land on the other products first. Re-check and consider a rename before any public launch beyond the hackathon.
