# RateSabi — Brand
- **Name:** RateSabi (one word, capital R and S)
- **Tagline:** "RateSabi knows the rate."
- **Why:** *Sabi* is Nigerian pidgin for know-how / "he sabi" = he really knows it. The agent's whole job is knowing the rate — better than the OTC desk chat, and proving it in the log.
- **Name check (2026-10-22):** no GitHub repo named `ratesabi`, `ahmardchain/ratesabi` free, no direct product hit in web search. Not a trademark clearance — re-check before any public launch beyond the hackathon.
- **Palette:** Ink `#070b12` (background), Panel `#0c1320`, Edge `#1c2a44`, Mint `#35e0b0` (primary / go), Gold `#f5c04e` (accents / prizes / caution), Muted `#8fa3bf`.
- **Voice:** Short, plain, receipts-first. Numbers before adjectives. Never claim a quote, user, or payment that didn't happen — say "indicative", "dry-run", or "wallet-gated" when that's the truth.
